Decision layer — buffer cost vs. naive baseline (24-month backtest)
Cost = idle carry (1×) + shortfall penalty (3×), ₹ cr. Baseline = hold last year's same-month outflow. Shortfalls = months the buffer was breached (forced distress selling).
Method. Target: AMFI monthly category net flows (Apr 2019–present, {N} months). Features: advance-tax / fiscal-year-end calendar flags, lagged net flow & same-month-last-year, RBI repo rate, Nifty-50 returns. Point + 80/95 intervals from the selected seasonal model; buffer from its predictive outflow distribution. The 1:3 cost asymmetry makes the cost-optimal buffer the 75th percentile of outflow; the 95th percentile is recommended as the operating point because it drives breaches to near zero at modest extra carry. Caveat: small sample (n≈90); the ML challenger (LightGBM-quantile) under-calibrates here and was not selected. Sources: AMFI, RBI, NSE. Not investment advice.
